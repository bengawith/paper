# Writing tone comparison — original V1 to Robust V2

The original V1 manuscript was read from `prior_final_draft.docx`. Its preferred academic register is direct and methods-first: **"We study..."**, **"We adopt..."**, **"We address..."**, followed by the measurements, the procedure, and the limits of interpretation. The vocabulary uses British English: *aerofoil, optimisation, parameterisation, behaviour, labelled*.

The revised abstract and introduction now follow that register. A repeated contribution list was removed; reviewer-process commentary in the main manuscript was replaced by the research motivation; the model was not retrained simply to improve a headline metric. The paper discusses measured R², grouped splits, actual XFOIL calculations, bounded perturbations and uncertainty without claims of formal reliability certification or demonstrated CFD fidelity.

V1 remains a **tone and historical methodology reference**, not the basis for importing its old lift-curve methods or scientific claims. The current study is a new point-conditioned CL/log-CD/CM surrogate with a trust-screened engineering application. The coauthors must approve the final style, claims, affiliations, authorship and target-journal requirements.
