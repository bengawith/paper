# Review status — 3 October 2026

**Read this before the manuscript. This package is a research draft for critical review, not a submission-ready or validated engineering release.**

Earlier assistant completion reports overstated the strength and readiness of this work. The manuscript, response letter, README and figure labels still contain claims requiring correction. The recorded data must take precedence over their interpretation in the prose. No new scientific experiments were run during review packaging.

## Priority corrections

1. **XFOIL effect-size sign is reversed in the manuscript.** `validation/xfoil_runner.py` computes reference drag minus candidate drag and explicitly records `positive_means_lower_drag_than_reference: true`. The negative values attributed to the deterministic and AVA designs therefore indicate higher candidate drag, not an improvement. The AVA design is also not the smooth-correlated robust optimum. The smooth-correlated optimum has no paired interval in the displayed summary. Claims that both optima are solver-verified improvements are not supported.
2. **Nominal rank correlation concerns only two successfully evaluated designs.** A Spearman correlation of 1 with two designs is not compelling evidence of general ranking reliability. Incomplete and failed cases must remain in the accounting, and complete-case selection bias must be discussed.
3. **Adversarial trust comparison has one fully trusted design.** The 0.043 versus 0.268 comparison is descriptive, not evidence that the trust rule generalises under adversarial search. The search ranks mean absolute lift gap; its reported `worst_case_overall` is not necessarily the largest single-angle discrepancy across all candidates (another inspected candidate had a 0.718 maximum). Initial candidates and refinement candidates must be counted separately: 49 initial plus 12 refinements, 20 rejected and 41 evaluated, of which 38 had matched data.
4. **Calibration coverage is not independent validation.** The approximately 95% coverage reported in the calibration artifact is measured on the data used to choose the band. It must not be described as proof of held-out coverage or conditional reliability. Group dependence and airfoil-versus-cluster bootstrap assumptions require review.
5. **Manufacturing statistics include violation penalties.** The raw field is named `weighted_cd_distribution_with_violation_penalty`; the report renames it. It must not be interpreted as an unpenalised physical drag distribution. CST refit rejection is not proof that a shape cannot physically exist, and a 1% CST-coefficient perturbation is not equivalent to 1% chord surface displacement.
6. **Model architecture description does not match the selected ensemble.** The 256-wide, three-layer SiLU model is the baseline. The tuning summary selects a 512-wide, two-layer ReLU model with approximately 0.122 dropout. Inspect the frozen ensemble manifest/checkpoints before finalising Methods. The development-subset MLP comparison is not the final ensemble comparison.
7. **Pareto terminology and statistical claims need correction.** The minimum expected-drag endpoint is not a deterministic (zero-uncertainty) optimum, neither endpoint is necessarily a knee, and concatenating seed fronts does not make their union globally nondominated. CVaR is a tail average, not worst-case drag. Seed-agreement failure remains unresolved. The working protocol explicitly is not a coauthor-approved preregistration.
8. **Validation and readiness remain limited.** No CFD or experimental verification was performed. The XFOIL retry aggregation needs a regression check ensuring nonzero-exit/timeout and invalid aerodynamic rows cannot be counted as successful coverage. Prediction input/geometry validation and frozen-artifact verification need hardening before a reliable release. Passing unit tests does not establish scientific validity.
9. **Timing is throughput, not interactive latency.** The 0.009 ms/state number is amortised ensemble prediction at batch size 6,144; it excludes loading, trust checking, geometry processing and uncertainty propagation. The measured single-state call was about 19 ms and a 48-state call about 22.5 ms. No end-to-end uncertainty-propagation speedup was measured.
10. **References and editorial review are incomplete.** Five new-method references were checked against arXiv pages in this session history; the entire bibliography was not independently verified. Reused references and bibliographic details require primary-source verification, as do the claims they support. The email correspondence states EAAI does not consider resubmission within a year of the 4 July 2026 rejection; do not assume a retitled manuscript bypasses that policy. Authorship, declarations, funding, AI-use disclosure and journal eligibility require author confirmation.
