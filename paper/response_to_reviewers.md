# Robust V2 — internal review resolutions

This is a factual record of independent audit findings and author revisions, **not** a purported response to actual journal referee comments. The prior response is preserved in the 21 October evidence archive.

1. **Original writing:** The full 9,244-word paper was rewritten from the author's exact original submitted Word document, retaining its engineering problem → data → method → results → discussion structure and explanatory first-person plural voice. Unsupported historical claims were not carried forward.
2. **Geometry labels:** The original coordinate-order defect affected 1,001 of 1,560 stored encodings. A new controlled dataset was generated by XFOIL on 1,256 exact CST reconstructions, admitting 106,931/141,928 actual numerical states without imputation.
3. **Model integrity:** Five neural members were retrained with geometry-cluster out-of-bag stopping, followed by calibration and a protected historical numerical test of 15,179 observed states.
4. **Prospective numerical reliability:** A sealed 200-geometry confirmation cohort was chosen before training and predicted before its XFOIL labels. Of 22,600 requests, 17,968 were observed. Disagreement-only AUROC 0.821 exceeded combined 0.802; the empirical operational threshold was not changed.
5. **Optimisation:** Both full ten-seed robust 128 × 200 × 64 and ten-seed 128 × 200 constrained nominal studies were completed. Feasibility and finite numerical agreement are not physical/global optimality claims.
6. **Selected solver evidence:** Every one of 819 strict local XFOIL cases was attempted. Only 290 were usable and 529 remain unavailable. No complete strict paired nominal comparison exists; paired 0.5% estimates have small explicit denominators.
7. **References:** All 29 numbered sources are real and supported by live DOI or publisher/author/conference records. The 2006 Kulfan–Bussoletti Crossref spelling error is resolved using the authors' actual PDF. Both historical book editions were checked.
8. **Visual integrity:** The standalone Word paper has seven tables and eight figures, with all 21 pages inspected in a content-derived browser preview. Figure caption grouping was repaired. Native Word pagination was not independently established.
9. **Outstanding CFD:** The entire independent viscous CFD verification protocol is written; the actual CFD outcomes and final physical aerofoil ranking cannot be completed without true CFD data. Authorship and disclosure approval is for the human coauthors, not an inferred review outcome.
