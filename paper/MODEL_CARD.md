# Controlled numerical model card

Lineage robust-v2-controlled-20261020. Five point-conditioned residual MLPs map twelve CST parameters and incidence to CL, log CD and CM. True local XFOIL labels on exact reconstructed input geometries were used for training. Source data has 106,931 observed and 34,997 missing/invalid states. All member weights and scalers, split assignments and calibration outputs are SHA256-frozen.

This model is fit for cautious reproducible XFOIL-family numerical prediction and constrained design research only. Its held-out historical identities have been inspected, and the sealed confirmation uses source-shape mixtures. The support/ensemble score does not certify conditional/adaptive coverage. This model has not been independently validated by RANS CFD, manufacturing experiments or flight tests.
